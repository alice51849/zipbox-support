What Zipbox opens and what it does not (check before you buy)

Zipbox is a one-time purchase, so here is the honest list: what it opens, what it cannot open yet, and what to check when an archive will not open.

Opens

  • ZIP and ZIPX, including ZIP64, split ZIP (.z01 + .zip) and password-protected ZIP
  • RAR and RAR5, including password-protected and split RAR (.part1.rar, or .rar + .r00)
  • 7z, including AES-256 encryption, encrypted file lists and split 7z (.7z.001)
  • TAR, GZIP, TAR.GZ, BZIP2, TAR.BZ2, XZ, TAR.XZ, LZMA, LZ4
  • ISO disc images (ISO 9660)

Does not do (yet)

  • Standalone .zst (Zstandard) and .cab files: Zipbox recognises them and tells you the format is not supported
  • Create RAR files: Zipbox creates ZIP and 7z only (RAR creation is not allowed by the RAR licence)
  • Archives with more than 50,000 entries are stopped for safety

If an archive will not open

  1. Split archive: put every part in the same folder and open the first part (.part1.rar, .7z.001 or the .zip that comes with .z01). Zipbox names the part that is missing.
  2. Asks for a password: the password comes from the sender; Zipbox cannot recover a lost password.
  3. Download not finished: compare the file size with what the sender expected and download it again.
  4. Garbled names in an old ZIP: change the file-name encoding (Big5, Shift-JIS, GBK, EUC-KR, CP437) in Settings.
