How do I look inside a ZIP, RAR or 7z file on iPhone and pull out only the one file I need?

Zipbox opens RAR, 7z, ZIP and 10 other archive formats right on your iPhone. Look inside before you extract, preview images, video, PDF, text and code, then take out only the files you need.

  1. Open an archive straight from Files, Safari downloads, Mail or the share sheet, including a RAR you downloaded in Safari that is just sitting in Files.
  2. See the list of what is inside without extracting, and preview images, video, PDF, text and source code.
  3. Search by file name and by text inside the archive (PDFs by file name only) to spot the one PDF among forty files.
  4. Extract one file, a selection or everything, then export to Files and iCloud Drive.
  5. Password-protected and multi-part ZIP, RAR and 7z archives open too. All 13 formats are handled on the device.

Zipbox makes no network requests and needs no account, with no analytics or tracking code. Zipbox is a paid app on the App Store: one purchase includes every feature, with no subscription and no ads.
