How to open a password-protected ZIP on iPhone

The standard approach is simple: get the password from the person who sent the file, then enter it in an extractor that supports password-protected archives. The built-in Files app may refuse some encrypted ZIP files or show an error, depending on the encryption method and your iOS version. If you do not know the password, asking the sender is the only right move. This page does not cover cracking or bypassing passwords.

First: where is the password?

In work settings it is common to send the ZIP in one email and the password in a second one. Before you try anything else, check:

  • Another message from the same sender, sent just after the attachment, often with “password” in the subject
  • A text message or chat where it may have been sent separately
  • A shared password your team or company agreed on in advance

If you still cannot find it, reply and ask: “Could you send me the password for the attachment?” Please do not guess repeatedly or use cracking tools. You could be breaking the protection on someone else’s files, and it is far slower than simply asking.

Try the built-in Files app

Some encrypted ZIP files open in the Files app, depending on your setup. It costs nothing to try:

  1. Save the ZIP to Files.
  2. Tap it.
  3. If a password prompt appears, enter the password.

If nothing happens, you see an error, or you get an empty folder, Files probably does not support that encryption method. At that point, use an extractor that supports password-protected archives.

Steps with an extractor app (shown with Zipbox)

  1. In Files, touch and hold the ZIP (or RAR or 7z), choose Share, then pick Zipbox. You can also open it straight from a Mail attachment.
  2. When asked, enter the password the sender gave you.
  3. When the file list appears, extract only the files you need.

Zipbox supports password-protected ZIP, RAR and 7z. It processes everything on the device and makes no network requests, so the files are not sent anywhere.

Typing tips: passwords are case sensitive. Watch for a trailing space picked up when copying from a message, and for look-alike characters such as 0 and O, or lowercase l and 1. If the password came in a different keyboard language, check for full-width characters too.

AES-256 vs ZipCrypto

There are two common kinds of ZIP password protection: the older ZipCrypto and the stronger AES-256.

MethodWhat it isOpening it
ZipCrypto (legacy)The original ZIP encryption, widely supportedOpens in most apps
AES-256Modern, much stronger encryptionNeeds an app with AES support

With the correct password, either kind opens. Without it, the archive should stay closed, which is exactly what encryption is for.

When you are sending a protected file

To send photos or documents more safely, you can create AES-256 encrypted ZIP or 7z archives in Zipbox. Share the password through a different channel than the file itself, such as a call or a chat message, rather than in the same email. If the recipient uses an iPhone, it is worth mentioning that the built-in Files app may not open encrypted ZIPs, so they may need an extractor app.

Pick a password that is long rather than clever: a few unrelated words strung together is easier to read out over the phone and harder to guess than a short string of symbols.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a ZIP if I forgot the password?

This page does not cover bypassing passwords. Ask the person who created or sent the file. If you made it yourself, looking for the original files is usually the realistic option.

The password is right, but it still will not open.

Check upper and lower case, full-width characters and trailing spaces. If it still fails, the download may be incomplete; save the file again.

Can password-protected 7z and RAR files be opened too?

Yes, with an app that supports them. Zipbox handles password-protected ZIP, RAR and 7z.

Why can I see file names but not open the files?

Standard ZIP encryption usually protects the contents but not the file names, so the list is visible while extracting still needs the password.
